Blood Group Compatibility Guide

Understanding who you can save. Your blood type determines who can safely receive your donation.

O- Negative

Universal Donor

O- blood can be given to patients of any blood type. It is crucially used in emergency rooms when a patient's blood type is unknown.

Can Give To: All Blood Types
AB+ Positive

Universal Receiver

Patients with AB+ blood can safely receive red blood cells from any blood type during a medical emergency or transfusion.

Can Receive From: All Blood Types

Can You Donate Blood?

Check out the basic eligibility criteria before you register or request for blood.

✔ General Requirements

  • Age: Must be above than 18 years old.
  • Weight: Must weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs).
  • Health: Must be in good general health at the time of donation and don't have any chronic disease like hepatitis, heart disease, diabetes and any kind of disease that is transmissible.
  • Hemoglobin: Minimum level of 12.5 g/dl for women and 13.5 g/dl for men.
  • Interval: At least 3 months (90 days) since your last donation.

✖ Who Cannot Donate

  • Illness: Active fever, flu, sore throat, or recent infections.
  • Medication: Taking antibiotics or specific prescribed drugs.
  • Travel: Recent travel to certain high-risk malaria areas.
  • Tattoos/Piercings: Got a new tattoo or piercing within the last 6 months.
  • Conditions: Chronic conditions like hepatitis, HIV, heart disease and any type of disease that is transmissible.
